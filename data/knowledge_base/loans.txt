TOPIC: Home Loan

ELIGIBILITY:
Minimum age: 21 years. Maximum age at loan maturity: 65 years.
Minimum income (salaried): Rs. 25,000 per month
Minimum income (self-employed): Rs. 3,00,000 per annum
Minimum CIBIL score: 700
Maximum loan amount: Up to 80% of property value (90% for loans below Rs. 30 lakhs)
Maximum tenure: 30 years

INTEREST RATES:
Floating rate: 8.5% to 10.5% depending on CIBIL score and loan amount
Fixed rate (first 3 years): 9.5% then converts to floating
Women borrowers get 0.05% concession on interest rate.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR HOME LOAN:
Identity proof: Aadhaar card or Passport or Voter ID
Address proof: Aadhaar card or utility bill (not older than 3 months)
Income proof (salaried): Last 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statement, Form 16 for last 2 years
Income proof (self-employed): Last 2 years ITR with computation, 12 months bank statement, business proof
Property documents: Sale agreement, title deed, approved building plan, NOC from builder
Photographs: 2 passport-size photos

PROCESSING:
Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount, minimum Rs. 5,000, maximum Rs. 25,000
Processing time: 7-10 working days after all documents are submitted
Disbursement: Direct to seller's account for resale property. Stage-wise for under-construction.

PREPAYMENT:
Floating rate loans: No prepayment penalty
Fixed rate loans: 2% penalty on prepaid amount if prepaid within 3 years

TOPIC: Personal Loan

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 21 to 60 years
Minimum income: Rs. 20,000 per month (net take-home)
Minimum CIBIL score: 700
Employment: Minimum 1 year in current job, 2 years total work experience

LOAN DETAILS:
Loan amount: Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 40,00,000
Tenure: 12 months to 60 months
Interest rate: 10.5% to 18% per annum depending on profile
Processing fee: 2% of loan amount plus GST

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR PERSONAL LOAN:
Identity proof: Aadhaar or PAN or Passport
Address proof: Aadhaar or utility bill
Income proof: Last 3 months salary slips, 3 months bank statement
Employment proof: Offer letter or employee ID card

DISBURSEMENT: Within 24 to 48 hours after approval.

TOPIC: Car Loan / Vehicle Loan

Loan amount: Up to 90% of on-road price for new cars. Up to 70% for used cars.
Tenure: 12 months to 84 months
Interest rate: 8.75% to 12% for new cars. 12% to 16% for used cars.
Processing fee: 1% of loan amount, minimum Rs. 2,500

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR CAR LOAN:
Identity and address proof (same as personal loan)
Income proof (same as personal loan)
Vehicle documents: Proforma invoice from dealer (for new car) or RC book (for used car)
Insurance: Comprehensive insurance mandatory

TOPIC: Education Loan

Loan amount: Up to Rs. 10 lakhs for studies in India. Up to Rs. 20 lakhs for abroad.
Interest rate: 9.5% to 13%
Moratorium period: Course duration plus 1 year (no EMI during this period)
Collateral: Not required for loans up to Rs. 7.5 lakhs. Required above that.
Tax benefit: Interest paid is fully deductible under Section 80E for 8 years.
