TOPIC: Online Banking and Transactions

NET BANKING:
To register for net banking: Visit home branch with Aadhaar and account number, or register online using debit card and OTP.
Daily transfer limit via net banking: Rs. 10,00,000 (NEFT/RTGS). Rs. 1,00,000 (IMPS).
First-time transfer to a new beneficiary: Limited to Rs. 25,000 for the first 24 hours (cooling period).

NEFT (National Electronic Funds Transfer):
Settlement: In batches, every 30 minutes, 24x7 including holidays.
Charges: Free for amounts up to Rs. 10,000. Rs. 2.5 for Rs. 10,001 to Rs. 1 lakh. Rs. 5 above Rs. 1 lakh.
Maximum amount per transaction: No upper limit.

RTGS (Real Time Gross Settlement):
Settlement: Real time, immediate.
Minimum amount: Rs. 2,00,000
Maximum amount: No upper limit.
Available: 24x7
Charges: Rs. 5 per transaction.

IMPS (Immediate Payment Service):
Settlement: Instant, 24x7 including holidays.
Maximum per transaction: Rs. 5,00,000
Charges: Rs. 5 per transaction up to Rs. 1 lakh. Rs. 15 above Rs. 1 lakh.

UPI:
Daily limit: Rs. 1,00,000 per day
Per transaction limit: Rs. 1,00,000
No charges for UPI transactions.
UPI PIN must be changed every 6 months for security.

TOPIC: Fraud and Disputed Transactions

REPORTING A FRAUDULENT TRANSACTION:
Step 1: Call customer care immediately at 1800-XXX-XXXX (24x7 toll free).
Step 2: Block your card and account if needed.
Step 3: File a complaint via net banking under "Dispute a Transaction" within 7 days.
Step 4: Visit home branch and submit written complaint with transaction details.

REFUND TIMELINE FOR DISPUTED TRANSACTIONS:
If bank error: Refund within 5 working days.
If merchant error: Refund within 7-10 working days after merchant confirmation.
If fraud confirmed: Provisional credit within 10 working days. Final resolution within 45 days.
If customer at fault (shared PIN etc): No refund applicable.

ZERO LIABILITY POLICY:
Customer has zero liability if: Fraud occurred due to bank negligence, transaction happened without customer knowledge and reported within 3 days.
Customer has limited liability if: Reported between 4-7 days after fraud — liable for up to Rs. 5,000.
Customer bears full liability if: Reported after 7 days, or customer shared PIN/OTP with fraudster.

TOPIC: Customer Support and Grievances

CUSTOMER CARE:
Toll free number: 1800-XXX-XXXX (24x7, all days)
Email: support@ourbank.com (response within 48 hours)
Branch visit: Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 4 PM (except 2nd and 4th Saturday)
Chat support: Available on net banking portal, 8 AM to 10 PM

GRIEVANCE ESCALATION:
Level 1: Customer care (resolve within 5 working days)
Level 2: If not resolved, escalate to Nodal Officer via nodal@ourbank.com (resolve within 10 working days)
Level 3: Banking Ombudsman — if bank does not resolve within 30 days, customer can approach RBI Banking Ombudsman at https://cms.rbi.org.in

KYC UPDATE:
KYC must be updated every 2 years for high-risk accounts, every 8 years for low-risk accounts.
Documents needed: Latest address proof and identity proof.
Can be done at branch, or via video KYC on the mobile app.
Account may be frozen if KYC is not updated by due date.

ACCOUNT CLOSURE:
To close an account: Visit home branch with passbook, debit card, and signed closure form.
All linked services (net banking, UPI, standing instructions) will be automatically disabled.
Any remaining balance will be paid by demand draft or transfer to another account.
Closure takes 5-7 working days.
